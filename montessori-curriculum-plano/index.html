Montessori Education

What is Montessori education?

Montessori education is a child-centered approach built around hands-on materials, purposeful work, independence, and careful guidance from teachers. On this page, Palm Grove applies that philosophy to a clear curriculum parents can understand by age, skill area, and daily rhythm.

Families often search for both what Montessori is and what children actually learn. At Palm Grove, those two questions come together in classrooms that are calm, organized, and intentionally prepared for each stage of development.

Curriculum Overview

A Montessori curriculum built for independence and kindergarten readiness.

Our curriculum is designed to support the whole child. We care about literacy and math, but also concentration, self-direction, social confidence, coordination, language growth, and the small daily habits that help children become capable. Each environment gives children the right level of challenge, freedom, and support for where they are right now.

A

Practical Life

Real-world skills that build coordination, focus, and independence

B

Sensorial

Refining the senses through hands-on exploration and classification

C

Language

Building literacy through phonics, vocabulary, and written expression

D

Mathematics

Concrete materials that make abstract concepts tangible and clear

E

Cultural Studies

Geography, science, art, and music to broaden understanding

Lessons are introduced individually or in small groups, allowing children to build confidence before moving forward. Each classroom keeps the same Palm Grove philosophy, but the goals, materials, and rhythm shift to match where children are developmentally.

🌿 Children don't just learn subjects — they develop the capacity to learn. 🌿

Curriculum by Age

How our Montessori curriculum changes by age.

Each classroom keeps the same Palm Grove philosophy, but the goals, materials, and rhythm shift to match the developmental stage of the children inside it.

Infant Class

Our youngest learners experience calm routines, sensory exposure, language-rich interaction, gentle movement, and secure attachment with caregivers in a peaceful environment.

Age6 weeks – 12 months

Mobile Infant

As children begin moving more independently, the curriculum supports coordination, exploration, language development, choice-making, and safe social discovery.

Age12 months – 18 months

Toddlers

Children strengthen practical life foundations through movement, language growth, social interaction, and hands-on exploration in a nurturing Montessori setting.

Age18 months – 24 months

Twos Class

Children begin practical life, early language, grace and courtesy, sensorial experiences, and the first steps of independence in a classroom made for active learners.

Age24 months – 2.5 years

Transition Class

This classroom supports the bridge into primary-level Montessori work by strengthening independence, focus, social confidence, and readiness for the 3-5 environment.

Age2.5 years – 3 years

Primary Montessori

Our preschool and Pre-K curriculum deepens literacy, math, practical life, cultural studies, concentration, and social confidence while preparing children for kindergarten.

Age3 – 5 years

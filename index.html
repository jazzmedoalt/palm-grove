Frequently Asked Questions — Montessori Preschool & Daycare in Plano

Yes — Palm Grove Montessori Academy is a Classical Montessori preschool located at 5900 Windhaven Parkway in Plano, TX. We offer Montessori-based early learning in a calm, prepared environment with certified educators, on-site swimming, multi-age classrooms, and designated learning sessions that support kindergarten readiness.

We serve children starting at 6 weeks to 6 years old, and we also offer a school-age program for after-school care and camps for school-age students.

Yes — Palm Grove offers daycare-style schedules with extended care from 6:15 AM – 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Our program is designed to support working families while keeping the day consistent and child-centered, with both full-time and part-time enrollment options available.

Montessori focuses on hands-on learning, independence, and a prepared environment where children learn at their own pace through purposeful work — rather than a one-size-fits-all, teacher-led model. At Palm Grove, we blend Classical Montessori principles with modern adaptations including STEM integration, progress tracking, and collaborative learning to prepare children for the K–12 environment.

At Palm Grove Montessori, children learn in a peaceful, calm environment that feels like home. Our warm and nurturing classrooms help children feel safe, confident, and ready to learn. What truly sets us apart is our amazing teaching team. Many of our teachers have been with us for more than 10 years, bringing experience, consistency, and genuine care for every child.

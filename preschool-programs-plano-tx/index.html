Our Programs

Preschool & Pre-K Programs in Plano, TX

Palm Grove Montessori Academy offers preschool programs in Plano, TX for children from infancy through Pre-K. Our early childhood education programs blend Classical Montessori work cycles with gentle, structured learning sessions that build school readiness—literacy, early math, independence, and social-emotional growth. Each program is tailored to a different stage of childhood while still building the independence, confidence, and readiness Palm Grove families value most.

Infant Program

Age: 6 weeks-18 months

In our Infant Program, we create a nurturing environment where babies are encouraged to explore the world around them through their senses. Guided by Montessori principles, infants absorb language, movement, and emotional connection while building trust, security, and the earliest foundations for independence.

Sensory discoverySafe, calm exploration designed for early brain and body development.
🫶
Secure attachmentWarm routines and responsive caregiving support trust and emotional safety.
🗣️
Language exposureDaily conversation, songs, and naming support early communication.
🌱
Early independenceThoughtful setup encourages babies to move, observe, and engage with confidence.

Infant program highlights

  • How routines support feeding, movement, and rest
  • Why a calm prepared environment matters even in infancy
  • How early Montessori supports confidence from the beginning

Families can expect a nurturing start that honors each baby's pace while supporting movement, connection, and early exploration.

Toddler Program

Age: 18 months - 3 years

Our Toddler Program is designed to harness sensitive periods for language, movement, and socialization. Children engage in hands-on work that promotes practical life skills such as dressing, feeding, cleaning, concentration, and growing autonomy in a thoughtfully prepared environment.

🧺
Practical lifeSmall daily tasks help toddlers build pride, coordination, and self-help skills.
🚶
Movement + orderChildren learn by doing, repeating, carrying, sorting, and exploring with purpose.
💬
Language growthRich vocabulary and guided interaction support emerging speech and comprehension.
✨
Confidence buildingGentle guidance helps children solve problems and do more for themselves.
🌴 🌴

Toddler program highlights

  • A toddler program that values independence without rushing children
  • Hands-on Montessori-style learning instead of overstimulation
  • Support for routines, early social development, and language

This stage gives toddlers the right balance of structure and freedom so they can practice independence in meaningful, age-appropriate ways.

What makes the program different

Montessori +, Palm Grove's modern approach

Palm Grove builds on traditional Montessori principles while also supporting the skills children need in today's educational environment. These four elements help shape that approach.

🧭

Individualized Learning Paths

Palm Grove tracks progress to better understand each child's strengths and struggles, then supports learning with experiences that match the next stage of growth and readiness.

🤝

Collaborative Learning + Social-Emotional Development

Children build confidence, empathy, flexibility, and classroom-ready social skills through shared work, guided interaction, and an environment that values community.

🔬

STEM and Technology Integration

Hands-on exploration remains central, while children are also introduced to modern tools and learning experiences that reflect today's educational landscape.

🌍

Cultural Awareness and Global Citizenship

Palm Grove emphasizes diversity, stewardship, and connectedness so children grow with curiosity not only about materials and subjects, but also about people and the world.

Program FAQs

Preschool Program FAQs

Quick answers to help families choose the right age-group program and next steps.

What ages do Palm Grove programs serve?

We serve children starting at 6 weeks through 6 years old in year-round programs, plus school-age options for after-school care and camps.

How are children placed in each program?

Placement is based on age and developmental readiness, so children are in a classroom that fits both their stage and learning needs.

What are your operating hours?

Our school operates Monday through Friday from 6:15 AM to 6:30 PM.

Do you offer school-age care and summer programs?

Yes. We offer school-age care and summer camp options with structured routines, hands-on activities, and a supportive environment.

How do we start enrollment?

The best first step is to schedule a tour so you can confirm fit, availability, and the right classroom path for your child.

See which program fits your child best

For most parents, the next step is seeing the environment in person. Schedule a tour to compare age groups, ask questions about routines and readiness, and decide which Palm Grove program feels like the right fit.

A Day at Camp

A Typical Summer Day at Palm Grove

Parents often ask, "What do they actually do all day?" Here's the rhythm:

Morning Arrival + Warm Welcome

A calm, friendly start that sets the tone for the day

Hands-On Learning Blocks

Projects, activities, and small-group work that spark curiosity

Enrichment & Specialty Classes

STEAM Room, Spanish, Summer Swim, and more

Outdoor Time + Movement

Fresh air, active play, and physical development

Lunch + Reset

A chance to refuel and recharge for the afternoon

Afternoon Activities

Creative work, collaboration, practical skills, and games

Closing Routines

Helping kids transition smoothly back to home

It feels active—but not chaotic. Engaging—but not overstimulating. The kind of day where kids come home tired in the best way.

Enrichment

Enrichment & Specialty Classes

Summer is when kids can explore interests that don't always fit neatly into a regular school day. Our specialty offerings add variety and excitement—while still aligning with how children learn best.

STEAM Room

Our STEAM Room includes science, technology, engineering, art, and music through hands-on projects, experimentation, and creative problem-solving.

Spanish Classes

Our Spanish program introduces children to a second language through music, stories, games, and everyday vocabulary. It's a playful and immersive approach that encourages early language acquisition and cultural appreciation.

Summer Swim

In partnership with certified instructors, our swim program introduces water safety and basic swimming techniques in a fun and supportive setting. Confidence and coordination grow as children develop essential life skills in the pool.

Our Classrooms

Infant Care for Non-Mobile and Mobile Infants

Specialized environments for each developmental stage—because a 3-month-old and a crawling 10-month-old need very different things.

Ages 6 Weeks–12 Months

Non-Mobile Infants

A calm, nurturing space focused on sensory exploration, predictable routines, and building secure attachments with primary caregivers. Gentle one-on-one interactions and age-appropriate materials support healthy brain development from day one.

What your baby experiences

  • Plenty of cuddles, eye contact, and responsive conversation
  • Tummy time, visual tracking, and soft sensory materials
  • Individual feeding and sleep schedules that mirror home
  • Consistent caregivers who learn your baby's cues
Ages 12–18 Months

Mobile Infants

For little ones on the move, this classroom supports early motor development, curiosity, and growing independence. Low shelves, open floor space, and soft climbing structures invite safe exploration at each child's own pace.

In this classroom, infants enjoy

  • Safe spaces for crawling, pulling up, and early walking
  • Hands-on materials that encourage problem-solving
  • Simple songs, stories, and finger plays for early language
  • Gentle support practicing independence during meals

Infant Care FAQs

Our infant program welcomes babies from 6 weeks through 18 months. We have two classrooms—one for non-mobile infants (roughly 6 weeks–12 months) and one for mobile infants (roughly 12–18 months). Placement is based on developmental stage as well as age. Schedule a tour to learn about current openings.

Our infant care is Montessori-inspired—the environment and caregiving approach support independence, calm, and natural development through respectful, responsive care rather than formal lessons. Learn more about our approach on the Our Program page.

Yes. We work closely with families to mirror home routines as much as possible, including breastmilk, formula, and nap preferences. Your baby's comfort and consistency always come first—we'll talk through the details during your tour.

We use a parent communication app where families receive updates and photos throughout the day, and they can communicate with the teacher at any time. Contact us anytime with questions.

During enrollment, we provide a clear checklist based on your child's needs—diapers, wipes, bottles, extra clothes, comfort items, and anything specific to their routine. We make the transition as simple as possible.

You can schedule a tour online or call us directly at (409) 403-5040. Tours are flexible, no-obligation, and you're welcome to bring your baby. It's the best way to see our infant rooms, meet the caregivers, and ask every question on your mind.
